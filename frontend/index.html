Built-in Intelligence

Four small innovations that add up

Smart Assignment

The right partner, every time

A transparent scoring heuristic weighs distance, active orders, and workload to recommend the best available delivery partner.

Order Batching

Fewer trips, faster kitchens

Nearby orders from the same restaurant are flagged as batching opportunities, saving distance with minimal added delay.

Food Waste Alerts

Stock that matches demand

A rule-based check compares inventory to expected demand and flags excess stock before it goes to waste.

Dynamic ETA

Estimates that stay honest

Delivery windows recalculate as prep time, partner assignment, and pickup status change in real time.
